02 Why it matters for a.team
TokenShelf · Proposal · May 2026

A trust layer no other
talent network can offer.

a.team sells elite AI talent measured by outcomes. Embedded as a.team’s productivity-proof layer, TokenShelf gives every engagement a receipt buyers can verify themselves — and gives builders credit for AI-native work that today gets dismissed as “just using a tool.”

01
A defensible differentiator.
No other talent network can prove its builders are more productive with AI — and no other network’s builders deliver engagements with materially lower AI spend by default. The moat: cost savings that pull adoption, network consent that earns trust, open code that anyone can verify.
02
Buyers verify the math themselves.
Every receipt is cryptographically signed. Buyers don’t have to take a.team’s word for it — they re-verify independently. The signal works even if TokenShelf or a.team disappears tomorrow.
03
A portable certified badge builders own.
Builders who participate earn an official certified badge with their score — signed, shareable, and theirs to keep. It travels across LinkedIn, portfolios, and future engagements. A real reason to stay on a.team’s network, especially for the AI-native builders other networks are actively recruiting.
What a.team sees

Per-engagement metrics

  • Time invested per session & engagement
  • Model spend ($) & tokens used — input, output, cache
  • Overall score (%) from objective signals
  • Account-manager view for retention & renewal
  • Never: prompts unless the builder explicitly shares them
What buyers see

With builder consent

  • Same metrics for the engagement they paid for: time, $, tokens, score (%)
  • Receipts & certified badge they can re-verify themselves
  • Session-level breakdown only if the builder approves
  • Never: prompts unless the builder chooses to show them

Builder decides what’s visible — score, prompts, everything. Show or hide per audience, anytime, from a single command.
